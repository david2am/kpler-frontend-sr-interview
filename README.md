# Kpler Senior+ Frontend Engineer - Technical Exercise

## Scenario

You are presented with the following situation:

A team of three engineers is tasked with launching a new product in the form of a small application. 

> [!NOTE]
> You can refer to [the product specifications provided by the product manager](product-spec.md).

The team consists of:

- 1 Junior Engineer
- 1 Mid-level Engineer
- 1 Senior Engineer (who has been unavailable for personal reasons during the development phase)

The team worked some time on the project and declared it was ready. The Product Manager (PM) and stakeholders have validated the feature set and the application's look and feel.

However, engineering management wants you to assess the technical quality of the project. As a trusted Senior+ Engineer, they rely on you to determine the quality standards required for production.

## Your Task

You are asked to:

1. Review the codebase.
2. Identify any quality issues that must be addressed before moving the product into production.
3. Provide comprehensive feedback and detailed guidance to the team to ensure the codebase is ready for integration into a mature codebase and can accommodate new developers.

The team has two weeks left to deliver the product. Your guidance is crucial to help them meet the quality standards expected for a production-ready codebase.

## Deliverable: a documented pull request

- Create a new branch and a new pull request.
- Use the **pull request description** to explain and guide the team on how to match your quality expectations for this project. List every point that needs improvement, with a general explanation.
- Use the PR **branch** to **implement code changes** that illustrate each kind of improvement you are suggesting in the description (no need to repeat the same improvement multiple times in different places in the code. Do it once for a great example, and mention the other places if any).
- You might need a detailed explanation for specific changes you suggest; let's use the **PR code review comments** for that purpose. Reserve in-code comments for comments you would like to keep in the codebase.

## Steps to Follow

1. **Fork the Repository**: Fork the repository into your own GitHub account.
2. **Detach your fork**: Detach the repository from the parent.
3. **Do your magic**: Review the code, and work on the deliverable.
4. **Share Access**: Share an invite to give us read access to your repository.

> [!NOTE]
> Don't be too much concerned if the team's implementation doesn't exactly match these specifications; the product manager has already approved the team's output.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Please don't forget to detach your fork from our main repo and then give us access to your repository.
> Send the invite to specific people we shared with you by email, and at least to @rbecheras and @Kharrisonkpler.

## Time Frame

You have 120 minutes to review the repository and send us your results.

Be yourself, and use this opportunity to demonstrate your quality expectations for a production-ready codebase, and your mentoring skills to guide your team members.
